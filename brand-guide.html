01Overview

A brand built on the roofline.

Crosby Roofing & Seamless Gutters is a Columbia-area roofing and gutter contractor. The existing mark already does the hard work: a house roofline sitting directly over a bold, high-contrast wordmark, resting on a gutter-shaped base that carries the service line. This guide documents that system so it stays consistent across trucks, signage, print, and social — and flags where a designer should confirm exact values against your master art files.

Local

Family-run, on the ground in the Midlands — not a national franchise. Every touchpoint should read as a real local crew, not a call center.

Sturdy

Bold block lettering, thick outlines, high contrast. The mark reads at a glance from the road on a truck door or a yard sign.

Direct

No jargon, no soft-sell. Roofing and seamless gutters, done right, explained plainly.

Don't break up the lockup

Icon, wordmark, and gutter bar move as one unit. Never run the wordmark without the roofline, or vice versa.

Clear space & minimum size — keep open space around the lockup equal to the height of the "C" in CROSBY on every side. Don't run the full lockup narrower than about 1.25″ on print, or below 90px wide on screen; drop to the roofline icon alone for favicon-scale uses.
03Color

Palette

Two reds carry the brand: a slightly deeper red for the wordmark itself, and a hotter red-orange for environments — the accent wall color already used in the shop and on interior signage. Values below are sampled from existing photography and signage; confirm exact prints/Pantone against your master vector files.

06Voice & Tone

How Crosby talks

Straightforward and local — a neighbor who happens to know roofing, not a call-center script. Use this as a starting point and adjust to match copy you're already running on the site and socials.

Say it like this

  • "We'll get up there, take a look, and tell you straight what it needs."
  • "Seamless gutters, cut and formed on site to fit your house."
  • "Family-owned, working the Midlands."

Not like this

  • "Industry-leading, best-in-class roofing solutions."
  • "Synergistic exterior enhancement services."
  • Anything that reads like it was written for a national franchise.

08Next Steps

To make this production-ready

No original master file exists for this logo — this guide and the PNGs in assets/logo/ are a from-scratch recreation built to match the existing signage and jobsite photos closely. That makes them the current working standard. If any of the following ever turns up, it's worth a refresh:

A designer-built master file

Having a proper vector (AI/EPS/SVG) built once — from this recreation or from scratch — would replace the hand-rebuilt version here and make every future use (signage, apparel, print) sharper and easier to resize.

Exact color values

Pantone/CMYK/hex from your sign vendor or printer for the wordmark red and the accent red-orange, if you have them on file.

Licensed brand fonts, if any

If the sign shop used a specific licensed typeface for the wordmark rather than a lookalike, note it here — Anton/Barlow above are a close open-source stand-in.

Current contact & taglines

Phone, service area copy, and any tagline you already run on the site/socials, to replace the placeholders in the Applications section.
